Fashion E-Commerce Meta Ads Tracking
Rainfall Media · Meta Media Partner

Scaling a Fashion Brand to 6.2x ROAS After iOS 14 Broke Their Tracking

🔴 The Problem

The brand was running Meta ads with ROAS dropping month-over-month after the iOS 14 update. Their pixel-based tracking was underreporting conversions by an estimated 40–60%, causing the algorithm to optimize blindly. Budget was being wasted on audiences that weren't converting.

⚙️ The Approach

  • Audited the full tracking stack — identified pixel firing gaps and event deduplication failures
  • Implemented Server-Side GTM with first-party data signals to bypass browser restrictions
  • Set up Meta Conversions API (CAPI) with event match quality score optimization — achieved EMQ score of 7.2/10
  • Rebuilt GA4 property with custom events and audience exports back to Meta
  • Restructured campaign architecture: Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns layered with manual CBO for prospecting
  • Launched iterative creative testing — 3 hooks × 3 bodies × 2 CTAs per week

✅ Results

6.2x ROAS achieved
~40% More conversions reported
$15K Monthly budget managed

E-Commerce Full-Funnel CRO
Chandrona · Digital Advertising Manager

Full-Funnel Creative Strategy That Cut CPC 20% While Boosting Conversions 35%

🔴 The Problem

Chandrona had a high CPC and stagnant conversion rate. Ad creatives were recycled — same formats, same messages, no systematic testing framework. The funnel had no middle — users went from cold awareness ads directly to product pages and bounced.

⚙️ The Approach

  • Built a 3-stage funnel: TOFU (problem-aware content) → MOFU (social proof & comparison) → BOFU (urgency & offer)
  • Introduced structured creative testing with weekly velocity: new hooks tested every 7 days
  • Segmented audiences by purchase intent signals — warm audiences retargeted with different creative angles than cold
  • Launched TikTok as a supplementary channel for TOFU — UGC-style content lowered CPM by 30%
  • Optimized landing page alignment — matched ad creative messaging to landing page copy, improving Quality Score

✅ Results

+35% Conversions up
+20% Total revenue

AI Automation Real Estate n8n
Automation Project · Zillow Lead Pipeline

Automated 1,000+ Real Estate Leads/Week — Eliminating 95% of Manual Data Entry

🔴 The Problem

A real estate team was manually copy-pasting Zillow property listings into their CRM — an hours-long daily process prone to errors and delays. By the time leads were entered, some had already gone cold. The team needed a hands-free pipeline.

⚙️ The Approach

  • Built a ZeroWork scraper targeting Zillow's property listing pages — extracting address, price, contact info, listing agent, and days-on-market
  • Data piped into n8n workflow for cleaning, deduplication, and enrichment
  • n8n auto-categorized leads by price range, location, and listing freshness
  • Integrated with CRM via API — leads auto-assigned to agents based on territory rules
  • Slack notification bot alerts agents within 60 seconds of a new matching listing
  • Weekly Google Sheets report auto-generated for team review
